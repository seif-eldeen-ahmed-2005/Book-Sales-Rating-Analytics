# 📚 Book Sales & Rating Analytics Dashboard

A robust dual-faceted Power BI analytical solution focusing on book market dynamics, publisher revenues, rating distributions, and author performance indicators.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project merges publishing metrics with reader review data to evaluate what drives book sales ($1.99M total gross sales, 10M units sold) and high ratings across various genres and historical phases.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Pages
1. **Book Sales Analytics:** Tracks top-performing publishers, gross sales by author, genre distribution, and historical phase performance.
2. **Rating Analytics:** Analyzes book ratings vs. price distributions, author rating spreads, language demographics, and top-rated titles (e.g., *Words of Radiance*).

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema (Relational modeling connecting books, authors, publishers, and sales tables)
* **DAX & Power Query:** Advanced measures for weighted average ratings and revenue aggregations.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
* **Top Publishers:** Digital services and major publishing houses dominate gross sales totals.
* **Rating Correlation:** Price vs. Rating scatter plots reveal stable reader satisfaction independent of book price points.
